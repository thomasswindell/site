Thomas Swindell

My name is Thomas Swindell and I'm a musician, instrument maker, and sculptor. Within these disciplines, I am interested in listening to, reacting to, creating, emulating, and augmenting sound. In live performance, I tend to gravitate towards experimentation and improvisation as I feel these strategies are the most effective in creating genuine reactions both to myself and to others and I would rather not impose any unnecessary artificial limitations on these reactions. My sculptures and instruments are more or less the same thing in that they are objects that make sounds either through human or environmental manipulation, although I make that distinction to denote the amount of thought put into the visual aesthetics of the object. With these objects, I am exploring the physical properties of sound and experimenting with reaction to sound on a more scientific level. While I create as an outlet for my own emotions, I do not intend my work to be a reflection of these emotions, they are simply what drive me to create. While I often subconsciously prescribe post facto meaning to my own work and the work of others, I do not intend any underlying meaning in my work, although I take no issue with peoples' personal interpretations.
